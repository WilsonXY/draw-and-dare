## Collaboration & Working Style

1. **Big Picture First**
   - Always present the high-level concept, architecture, and intuition before diving into code or granular implementation details.
   - Explain the "why" and overall workflow first; proceed to deeper technical mechanics once the foundation is clear.

2. **Keep It Stupidly Simple (KISS)**
   - Favor minimal, straightforward, and readable solutions over elaborate abstractions or premature optimizations.
   - Break multi-step problems down into small, easily digestible pieces.

3. **Progressive Depth**
   - Start with simple working implementations or prototypes.
   - Layer complexity incrementally as understanding and requirements evolve.
